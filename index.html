Jobsoo — AI Job Recommendation Portal

Live Streamlit Application
Jobsoo - AI Job Recommendation Portal
Open Live App

Jobsoo is an AI-powered portal designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and relevant career openings. Powered by Streamlit and the Groq Kimi K2 AI inference engine, it parses PDF and text resumes, extracts structured skill trees, performs semantic similarity matching, and provides instant recommendation rankings.

Key Architectural Highlights:

  • Dynamic Resume Parsing: Ingests unstructured candidate text into clean skill vectors.
  • Groq Kimi K2 LLM Engine: Ultra-fast reasoning and contextual role matching.
  • Streamlit UI Interface: Clean, responsive data dashboard with interactive candidate telemetry.
  • One-Click Application Dispatch: Automated candidate routing to hiring pipelines.
